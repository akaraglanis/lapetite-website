About La Petite

A tiny studio in Marathon, with a whole lot of sprinkle.

La Petite started in a home kitchen with one stand mixer, a well-loved piping bag and a stack of birthday orders for friends. Today it's still a one-baker boutique studio in Marathon, Greece — small on purpose, so every order gets the time and care it deserves.

I bake in small batches with proper butter, real vanilla and seasonal fruit, then decorate by hand. Every piece is made to be the centrepiece of a small celebration — a little wonky on purpose, always full of personality.
